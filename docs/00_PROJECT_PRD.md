# Project Nawab
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Planning  
**Owner:** Praneet  
**Project Type:** Premium Digital Wedding Experience

---

# 1. Vision

Project Nawab is a luxury, mobile-first digital wedding experience.

This is NOT a traditional wedding website.

It is NOT a simple invitation.

It is an immersive digital experience that welcomes guests, tells our story, helps them plan their journey, manages RSVP, and acts as a digital concierge throughout the wedding.

The website should feel handcrafted, premium, emotional and unmistakably Indian while remaining elegant and modern.

When guests open it, the reaction should be:

> "I've never seen a wedding invitation like this."

---

# 2. Target Audience

Primary Audience

- Friends from USA
- Friends from Canada
- Friends from India

Secondary Audience

- Family members
- Elderly relatives
- Wedding planners

Most guests will access the website from their mobile phones.

---

# 3. Product Goals

The website should:

- Create excitement about the wedding
- Make guests feel personally welcomed
- Help guests plan travel
- Introduce Lucknow beautifully
- Provide complete wedding information
- Make RSVP effortless
- Reduce WhatsApp confusion
- Become the single source of truth for guests

---

# 4. Design Philosophy

The experience should feel like opening a handcrafted luxury wedding invitation.

Design should be:

- Elegant
- Timeless
- Warm
- Minimal
- Emotional
- Premium
- Editorial
- Sophisticated

Avoid flashy animations or clutter.

Every interaction should feel intentional.

---

# 5. Inspiration

Inspired by:

- The Leela Hotels
- Oberoi Hotels
- Sabyasachi
- Apple product storytelling
- Luxury magazines
- Handcrafted Indian wedding invitations
- Lucknow Nawabi architecture
- Chikankari craftsmanship

Not inspired by:

- Generic wedding templates
- Loud gradients
- Glitter
- Clipart
- Cartoon elephants
- Random peacocks
- Bright red backgrounds

---

# 6. Indian Identity

The Indian touch should be subtle and luxurious.

Use inspiration from:

- Chikankari embroidery
- Mughal arches
- Jaali patterns
- Handmade paper
- Antique gold detailing
- Floral line art
- Palace architecture
- Marigold accents where appropriate

Do not overwhelm the interface.

# 8. Typography

Headings

Elegant luxury serif.

Body

Typography should prioritize readability on mobile devices.

# 9. Mobile First

The entire experience must be designed for phones first.

Desktop should adapt naturally.

Everything must feel native on modern smartphones.

Editorial layout.

Large photos.

# 19. RSVP

Every guest receives a personalized URL.

Example:

/guest/john-smith

When opened:

"Welcome John & Emily"

Fields:

- Attendance
- Number of Guests
- Meal Preference
- Arrival Date
- Departure Date
- Flight Details
- Airport Pickup
- Hotel Requirement
- Special Requests

---
